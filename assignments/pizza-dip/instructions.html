STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

01

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll into smooth round balls.

02

Combine melted butter, parmesan, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Roll each dough ball thoroughly.

03

Arrange the seasoned dough balls along the rim of a greased heavy cast iron skillet.

04

Blend cream cheese, ricotta, and 2 tsp seasoning. Spread smoothly in the skillet center.

05

Pour marinara sauce evenly over the cheese blend without covering the surrounding dough.

06

Scatter shredded mozzarella and the remaining 1 tsp seasoning over the rich tomato sauce.

07

Top with cut pepperoni slices. Transfer to oven and bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbling.
