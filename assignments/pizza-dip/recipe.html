ARTISAN RECIPE

PIZZA DIP

Home Bread Ingredients Method Dip Base & Toppings

INGREDIENTS

Bread Elements

  • 16.3 oz Grands biscuit dough (8 count)
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

METHOD

01

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll into balls.

02

Combine melted butter, parmesan, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Roll each dough ball thoroughly.

03

Arrange the seasoned dough balls along the rim of a greased cast iron skillet.

04

Blend cream cheese, ricotta, and 2 tsp seasoning. Spread in the skillet center.

05

Pour marinara sauce evenly over the cheese blend without covering the surrounding dough.

06

Scatter shredded mozzarella and the remaining 1 tsp seasoning over the tomato sauce.

07

Top with cut pepperoni slices. Transfer to oven and bake for 25–30 minutes until bubbling.

DIP BASE & TOPPINGS

Ingredients List

  • 8 oz softened cream cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups grated mozzarella
  • 8 slices pepperoni (quartered)

CHEESE PULL

Rich melted center served piping hot

GOLDEN CRUST

Baked skillet with garlic butter bread
